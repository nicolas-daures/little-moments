For processing carried out as part of publishing this website, the controller is To be completed by the publisher before publication.. You can contact them at To be completed by the publisher before publication.. Further contact details are available in the legal notice.

The website is intended for parents and accompanying adults. It does not request user accounts, children’s names, dates of birth, photos or precise locations. No payment is required to use the website. Age filters only help select activity cards and are not saved by the website.

2. What stays in your browser

The website only uses storage for the functions described below, without advertising identifiers or profiling. Favourite and completed-activity lists are saved when you use the corresponding buttons.

Item Purpose and content Retention
Favourites Identifiers of the activity cards you choose to find again later; local storage. Until you or your browser delete them.
Completed activities Identifiers of activity cards marked as already tried; local storage. Until you or your browser delete them.
Offline cache Copies of pages, activity cards, styles, images, fonts and application files so they can be viewed offline. Until replaced during an update or deleted through browser settings.
Search and filters Criteria used in the open page, without being sent to a server by the application. While the page is in use.
Chosen language French or English, saved when you use the language selector. No tracking identifier. Until you change language or delete the website’s data in your browser.

The application does not send your two lists to the publisher or to an analytics service. Anyone using the same browser profile on the same device may still see them. They are shared between the French and English pages on the same site, but are not synchronised between devices.

Back up or transfer your collection

Download a small backup of your favourites and completed activities. You can restore it on this device or another one. The file is created and read locally: it is never sent to the website.

Delete your lists on this device

You can remove each favourite or completed activity from its card, or delete both lists here. This does not delete the offline cache or your chosen language.

To delete offline files and your saved language as well, use your browser’s site-data settings and, if necessary, uninstall the application. Offline mode may then be unavailable until your next visit.

3. Cookies and external services

The code in this version does not set HTTP cookies and contains no automatically loaded analytics tool, advertising, embedded video or social-network widget. Fonts, images and other application resources are served from the same website.

The functional storage described above provides the saving features you request and offline operation; it is not used to track your browsing. No advertising-consent banner has therefore been added to this version.

Depending on the hosting provider’s security settings, technical security cookies may be needed. Their presence, purpose and lifetime must be checked on the actual published address. Any future tracking requiring consent must be preceded by appropriate information and a choice mechanism.

The Share button uses your system’s sharing service or copies a link at your request. The website does not load a social-network module. If you choose a third-party application or website, its own privacy rules apply.

Entirely optional support

If a “Buy me a coffee” button is available, it opens a Ko-fi page in a new tab. No Ko-fi widget or resource is loaded before you click, and the original page address is not sent. Support is for adults only and is never required to access activities.

Payment is handled on the external service under its own terms and privacy policy, together with those of the chosen payment provider. This website does not collect or store your card number. Some support details (display name, email, amount and optional message) may be accessible to the publisher in the payment dashboard to keep track of support and meet applicable obligations. Do not send any information about a child.
