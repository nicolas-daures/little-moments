1. A free service for families

Les petits moments offers activity ideas you can browse, filter, save locally, share by link or print for personal and family use. No purchase, account or subscription is required. There are therefore no terms of sale for this service.

These terms explain how the website works and the precautions for using it. You do not need to tick an acceptance box simply to browse activity cards.

A “Buy me a coffee” link may allow adults to support the project voluntarily via Ko-fi. Support does not unlock any content or benefits: activities remain free for everyone. Payment takes place on the external service under its own terms and is never required to use this website.

2. Choose a suitable activity

  • Activities should be prepared and carried out with an adult. Suggested ages are a guide: adapt actions, materials and duration to each child’s abilities.
  • Read the whole card before starting, check the available space and follow the manufacturers’ instructions for the equipment you use.
  • Leave difficult cutting, heat sources, dangerous actions and tasks unsuitable for the child to an adult. Keep small items out of young children’s reach.
  • For cooking, check allergies, dietary restrictions, hygiene, ingredient freshness, cooking and choking risks. Cooking times depend on your equipment.
  • Outdoors, consider weather, terrain, traffic and local rules. Do not taste unfamiliar plants or disturb animals.
  • Respect a child’s choice not to take part. For photos, videos or recordings, ask the people concerned for permission and protect children’s privacy.

Stop an activity if conditions are unsuitable or it causes discomfort. An activity card does not replace professional advice where a particular need requires it.
